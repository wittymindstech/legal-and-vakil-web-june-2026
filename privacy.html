Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and protect all legal documents and data with encryption.

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you proactively provide when registering on our Website, filling out inquiry forms, or booking consultations. This includes:

  • Full Name and contact details (email address, phone number).
  • Brief descriptions of your legal requirements or case details.
  • Billing and payment information processed by our secure payment partners.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Assign you to the most qualified independent lawyer or consultant for your case.
  • Facilitate booking sessions, confirm payments, and deliver consultation scheduling.
  • Respond to inquiries, provide user support, and improve the platform design.
  • Send automated consultation updates and legal newsletters (if subscribed).

3. Data Confidentiality & Privilege

We take confidentiality seriously. All details regarding cases, chats, document reviews, and communications with independent professionals remain strictly privileged. We enforce industry-standard security practices to prevent unauthorized access or sharing of your case data.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. Your details are shared exclusively with the assigned professional to complete the requested consultation or legal service. We may also share transactions with payment processors to complete checkouts safely.

5. Security Standards

We use encrypted communication protocols (HTTPS) and partner with PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways to process transactions. Although we use top security practices, no digital storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
